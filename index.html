Early detection

Early detection is early prevention

The CTC test finds circulating tumor cells in a small blood sample — built for patients who've completed chemotherapy and want to monitor recurrence, and for those with a family history who want peace of mind.

  • First in Malaysia to use Negative Selection Technology to isolate tumor cells from blood.
  • Screens using epithelial cancer markers across 21 common cancer types.
  • Non-invasive — only a small blood draw is required, no biopsy.
  • Results are typically ready within 2 to 3 working days.
